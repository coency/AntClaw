爬爬蚁 · 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-09-07 · 适用于 Chrome 扩展「爬爬蚁」/ Applies to the Chrome extension “爬爬蚁 (AntClaw)”

1. 引言 / Introduction

本扩展（「爬爬蚁」/ AntClaw）帮助用户把自己账号在 抖音创作者中心 与 微信视频号助手 的数据中心统计导出为 CSV（含作品封面），按 上一个月 / 上一周 周期统计。本政策说明本扩展如何处理信息。

This extension (AntClaw / 爬爬蚁) lets you export your own account's data-center statistics from Douyin Creator Center and WeChat Channels Assistant to CSV (including work covers), for the previous month / week. This policy explains how the extension handles information.

2. 我们收集什么 / What We Collect

我们不收集任何个人信息。本扩展不收集、不上传、不传输、不出售任何数据。

We collect nothing. The extension does not collect, upload, transmit, or sell any data.

  • 不采集：不采集账号密码、浏览记录、设备信息、IP 地址或任何可识别身份的数据。
  • No collection: No credentials, browsing history, device info, IP address, or any personally identifiable data.
  • 不上传：无远程服务器、无统计 / 埋点 / 遥测、无第三方 SDK。
  • No upload: No remote servers, no analytics, no telemetry, no third-party SDKs.
  • 本地处理：所有数据均只读地在您的浏览器本地处理，产物只导出到您自己的电脑。
  • Local only: Everything is processed read-only in your browser; results are exported only to your own computer.

3. 数据用途 / How Data Is Used

仅当您主动点击「提取数据」时，扩展才读取您本人已登录账号在官方平台页面上展示的统计数字，并将其整理为 CSV / 封面 ZIP 供您下载。除此之外不做任何其它用途。

Only when you click “提取数据 (Extract)” does the extension read the statistics shown for your own signed-in account on the official pages, and package them into CSV / cover ZIP for you to download. No other use is made.

4. 数据存储 / Data Storage

扩展仅在本地（浏览器 storage）保存最近一次导出结果与状态，用于在您关闭 / 重开弹窗后恢复下载按钮。这些数据不离开您的浏览器，仅本扩展可读，随扩展卸载一起清除。

The extension only stores the latest export result and status locally (browser storage), so you can recover the download buttons after closing/reopening the popup. This data never leaves your browser, is readable only by this extension, and is removed when the extension is uninstalled.

5. 权限说明 / Permissions

本扩展声明的权限及用途：

Permissions declared and their purposes:

  • 下载 downloads：把导出的 CSV 与封面 ZIP 保存到您电脑（仅点击下载按钮时）。
  • downloads: to save the exported CSV and cover ZIP to your computer (only when you click download).
  • 存储 storage：在本机保存导出结果与状态（见第 4 节）。
  • storage: to save the export result and status locally (see §4).
  • 通知 notifications：导出完成时弹一条本地通知（仅导出完成时一次）。
  • notifications: a single local notification when an export finishes.
  • 站点访问 host permissions：creator.douyin.com、channels.weixin.qq.com、*.douyinpic.com、finder.video.qq.com —— 仅为读取您已打开页面的数据与下载封面。
  • host permissions: creator.douyin.com, channels.weixin.qq.com, *.douyinpic.com, finder.video.qq.com — only to read the page you have open and to download covers.

注：本扩展不声明任何未使用的权限（如 scripting）。/ Note: No unused permissions (e.g. scripting) are declared.

6. 数据共享 / Data Sharing

本扩展不与任何第三方共享数据，不连接任何第三方服务器，不使用任何分析或广告服务。

The extension does NOT share data with any third party, does NOT connect to any third-party server, and does NOT use any analytics or advertising service.

7. 安全 / Security

由于所有数据均在您本机处理、且从不离开浏览器，不存在数据传输泄露风险。请仅在您本人或您有权管理的账号上使用本扩展。

Because all data is processed on your machine and never leaves the browser, there is no data-transmission exposure. Please use the extension only for your own account or accounts you are authorized to manage.

8. 您的权利 / Your Control

所有数据仅在您点击「提取数据」时读取，产物保存在您的电脑上，您可以随时删除。卸载扩展即清除其本地存储，不留任何数据。

Data is read only when you click “Extract”. Results are saved on your computer and can be deleted at any time. Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage and leaves no data behind.

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use indicates acceptance of the updated terms.
